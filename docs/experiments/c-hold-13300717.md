## Procedure

Loaded the saved C-contact candidate once and advanced physics for
3 seconds with constant actuator targets. Excluded the first 0.5
seconds from hold statistics. No joint-position resets or forearm
repositioning occurred during the rollout.

The intended contact was the ring fingertip against the A string
within the third-fret region. Normal forces were summed across
contact points belonging to that pair and region.

## Interpretation and limitations

The actuators maintained an initialized C-contact candidate in this
simulation. This is one diagnostic rollout, not evidence of reliable
approach, chord transitions, robustness, generalization, or RL success.

The 0.01 N force threshold and 95% persistence criterion are provisional
screening choices, not validated musical-note criteria. No force ceiling
was part of the pass criterion.

The instrument uses rigid contact proxies, not vibrating strings.
The forearm placement was selected for this C candidate and has not
been validated across all chords.

Mac MuJoCo 3.1.4 and CARC MuJoCo 3.8.1 differ. Research experiments
should use a pinned reference stack.
